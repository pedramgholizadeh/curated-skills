# Cinematic Promo Skills

Skills for cinematic, music-synced product and brand videos.

## Typical skill topics

- Beat-synced editing and pacing to a soundtrack
- Shot recipe cards: camera moves, lighting, lens language
- Remotion-based programmatic promo rendering
- Sound design and mix for short-form promos
