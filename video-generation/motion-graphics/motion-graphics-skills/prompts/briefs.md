# Briefs and designer notes

One line gets you surprisingly close. For something people actually share, give it a proper brief, then give it notes like you would a designer.

## The brief behind my "Why do we dream?" film

I picked a question everyone has asked and nobody can answer, because we all want to know and AI can explain it visually. This is exactly what I typed, with `vox-explainer` installed. Use it as a model for any "why" film:

```
Why do we dream? And then someone suddenly wakes up, zooms out of the eye, and goes into outer space. There are neural networks of interconnectivity to convey the brain.
```

It found a source for every fact before it drew a thing, wrote the script as spoken lines, added a voice and rendered it.

A good brief has the same three parts:

1. **The question** people half understand.
2. **The visual turn** that surprises (wakes up, zooms out of the eye, goes into space).
3. **The metaphor** that carries the idea (neural networks for the brain).

Use this to write your own in the same shape:

```
[The question your audience keeps asking]? And then [the visual turn: what happens on screen that nobody expects]. [The metaphor that shows the idea: what it looks like].
```

## Notes on the dream film

These are the two notes I gave it, word for word. It fixed both.

Use this when the opening does not earn the rest of the film:

```
the text at the start undersells what’s coming next
```

Use this when the voice is fighting the sound design:

```
you can’t really hear the audio on top of the sound effects
```

## Notes on my launch video

These are the four notes I gave on my LinkedIn AI OS launch video, word for word. Each note names one thing to change and leaves the rest alone.

Use this when it looks generic:

```
I wouldn’t say it looks very premium.
```

Use this when it has drifted from your colours:

```
This just isn’t in my brand colours.
```

Use this when the pacing drags before the payoff:

```
It needs a full stop at the end of ‘in your voice.’ It takes a while to get to the reveal.
```

Use this when the voiceover is the problem:

```
I hate the voice. Maybe we should just go for a nice backing soundtrack instead.
```

## How to give a note

- **One thing per note.** Name the part and what is wrong with it. Claude changes that and leaves the motion you liked alone.
- **Say how it feels, not how to fix it.** "It doesn't look very premium" works. You do not need the technical word.
- **Point at a moment when you can.** "The text at the start" or "at 3 seconds" tells Claude where to look.
